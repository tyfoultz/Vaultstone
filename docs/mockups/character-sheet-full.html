Personality Traits NEW
I feel more at ease around animals and plants than among people. I speak in nature metaphors that others find confusing.
Ideals NEW
Balance — all life is connected. I will not tip the scales of nature carelessly.
Bonds NEW
The Thornwood is my home. I will die before letting it fall to corruption.
Flaws NEW
I distrust cities and the people who build them. I have trouble reading social cues.
Backstory NEW
Raised in the ancient forest of Thornwood by a circle of Moon Druids, Kaela never knew her birth family. She learned to speak with beasts before she learned Common. At age 16 a blight began creeping from the east, corrupting the oldest trees. She left the forest for the first time to find its source...
Allies & Organizations NEW
The Circle of the Moon (Thornwood) — her druidic order. Elder Yaveth serves as mentor. The Leafwalkers — a ranger company patrolling the eastern border.

Session 12 — The Sunken Keep
Session Apr 18 · Edit
We followed the ranger Maren into the collapsed eastern wing of the keep. The blight was strongest there — the stone itself was crumbling, corrupted from within.

Beneath the main hall we found the wellspring. Ancient druidic inscription around its base — but twisted. Someone used old Thornwood magic to invert the cycle. Life energy being siphoned outward instead of inward.

Key clue: A sigil carved into the cap stone — same as the one on the medallion we found in Session 9. Maren recognized it but wouldn't say from where.

Next session: Confront Maren. Find the medallion's origin.
