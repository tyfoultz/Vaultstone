Vaultstone · Character Sheet Navigation
Tab Organization Wireframes

Four distinct approaches to organizing the character sheet. Each wireframe shows the mobile bottom tab bar and the web top tab bar side by side, with a content map showing what lives where.

Current gaps vs. 2024 D&D sheet: Spells (spell slots + spell list) are completely absent. Personality Traits, Ideals, Bonds, Flaws, and Backstory are not implemented. Senses (Darkvision, etc.) and Passive Investigation/Insight are missing from Skills tab. Death Saves UI exists but Allies & Organizations have no home.

Legend: Gold = new section (not yet built)  ·  White = already built  ·  Gray = sub-item
Option A Four Tabs — Spells Folded In

Keep the current 4-tab shape. Add a Spells section inside Combat (works for most classes — spells ARE combat resources). Add Personality/Backstory to Story. Minimal nav change, maximal content completion. Best for: non-casters and players who don't want a dedicated spell screen.

Option B Five Tabs — Dedicated Spells

Break Spells out into its own top-level tab. Casters get a full spellbook view; non-casters see an empty tab (or it's hidden). Combat slims down to pure combat resources. Best for campaigns heavy on spellcasters.

Option C Six Tabs — DDB-style

Mirrors D&D Beyond's tab structure: Actions · Spells · Inventory · Traits · Background · Notes. Maximally granular — every section has a clear home. Best for power users and DMs who need to find things fast. Heavy nav overhead for casual players.

Option D Three Tabs + Sub-Nav

Three high-level tabs with sub-navigation inside each. Top level is conceptually clean (Play · Character · Lore). Sub-tabs give you granularity without cluttering the main nav. Requires an extra tap to reach any sub-section.
