Personality Traits
Speaks to animals and plants as equals. Hums old elven tunes while keeping watch.
Ideals
The natural world must be preserved from those who exploit it for power.
Bonds
An ancient oak in the Thornwood that serves as a waypoint for Kaela's people.
Flaws
Distrustful of cities and their inhabitants.
Backstory
Raised in a druidic circle deep within the Thornwood, Kaela learned early that the forest speaks to those who listen. After a logging company threatened the ancient grove, she left to seek allies — and found the party.
