Acid Splash Conjuration

Time 1 action Range 120 ft Comp V, S Dur Instant

Time 1 minute Range Touch Comp V, S, M Dur Instant

Repair a single break or tear in an object you touch — a torn cloak, a broken chain link, a leaking wineskin.
