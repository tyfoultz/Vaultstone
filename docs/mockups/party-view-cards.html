Party-view cards — compact + expanded

Two card shapes for the campaign party view. Compact for dense at-a-glance scanning during a session, expanded for between-encounter context. Both consume the same character data and would update live via the existing Supabase Realtime channel (character_updates for HP / temp / conditions / spell slots, server-pushed inside the wizard / level-up / sheet edits already shipping today).

Compact card — what it surfaces, in priority order
  • Edge stripe turns red/orange/green based on HP %, so the DM can scan a wall of cards and see who's in trouble without reading numbers.
  • HP bar is the dominant element. Temp HP rides above as a blue pill, not folded into the same number.
  • AC / Speed / Init as a 3-cell strip — the second-most-asked stats at the table.
  • Concentration gets an explicit pill with the spell name. Crucial for DM (saves on hit) and for ally healers ("don't waste the heal").
  • Conditions render as warning pills only when present. Empty by default.
  • Death saves swap in when HP === 0, replacing the conditions row. Successes/failures pip-by-pip so the DM doesn't have to open the sheet.
  • Damage / heal flash appears top-right for 2–3s after a Realtime tick, then fades. The actual HP number updates with a brief highlight underneath.
  • Presence dot on the portrait shows online/offline (gray when the player closed the tab).

Expanded card — what it adds over compact
  • Five-cell stat strip (AC / Speed / Init / Prof / Insp) replaces the three-cell compact strip — same data, all visible without expansion.
  • Saves row (six mods) — DM can call out a Wisdom save and see who's at-risk without flipping sheets.
  • Spell slots render as pip-rows per level, only for casters. Drained slots show empty; the cleric's L1 row at ○●●● tells you they've burned 1 of 4 first-level slots.
  • Mini-log shows the last 2–4 events on this character — damage, heals, saves, ability uses. Drives "what just happened to Thistle?" answers at a glance.
  • Concentration can sit either as a chip in the HP block (Oz's Faerie Fire) or in the status row (Garruk's Second Wind ready) — both shown.
Open decisions react to any of these
How does a player toggle between compact and expanded?
  • Page-level toggle (one switch for all cards)
  • Per-card click-to-expand (hover on desktop, tap on mobile)
  • Auto-expand based on session state (dying → expanded; full HP → compact)
  • DM controls toggle for all players
What renders during combat vs out-of-session?
  • Out-of-session: hide damage/heal flashes, mini-log, death saves
  • In-session: surface initiative order on cards (highlight current turn)
  • Both: keep HP / AC / temp / concentration visible
Click target — what happens when you tap a card?
  • Opens the full character sheet
  • Opens a quick-action sheet (apply damage / heal / condition)
  • Toggles expanded state inline
  • DM-only: opens HP-modifier dialog directly
Privacy — what does each player see of the others?
  • All vitals public (current behavior on PCStub pages)
  • HP redacted to healthy / bloodied / down labels for non-DM viewers
  • DM sees full state; players see compact-only of their own party
