The Atlas — Layout Variations
Three alternatives, same framework
All variations keep the Atlas philosophy — always-visible context rails, tabbed center content, no hidden state during play. Each explores a different trade-off between info density, column width, and what lives where.
Variation 1
Adaptive Right Panel
The right rail changes to complement whatever tab is active. On Combat it shows Skills. On Spells it shows the full slot tracker and concentration banner. On Skills it shows senses and languages. The right rail always reinforces the active tab rather than being static — so no content is wasted.

Variation 2
Flipped Atlas
Skills live on the left because that's what the DM calls for most often ("give me a Perception check") — your eye naturally goes left first. Character stats and identity move to the right where you scan them less frequently. Center tab content gets the same space as before. The left-to-right read becomes: what can I do → what am I doing → who am I.

Variation 3
Minimal Left Rail
The left rail strips down to identity + vitals only — portrait, name, HP, and the four numbers you need every single turn (AC, Initiative, Speed, Proficiency). Ability scores and saves move into the Combat tab's first column where you actually use them to roll. This unlocks a wider center area that fits 3 columns of content instead of 2, giving Combat its most useful layout yet.
