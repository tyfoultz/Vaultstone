1st-Level Enchantment

Veilbinder's Snare

A whisper of geas curls through the air, cinching tight around a wandering mind.
Prepared Concentration Wizard Sorcerer
Casting Time
1 Action
Range
60 ft
Components
V, S, M*
Duration
Conc., 1 min

Choose one creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become ensnared by your geas-thread for the duration. While ensnared, the target's speed is halved, and it has disadvantage on attack rolls against any creature other than you.

At the end of each of its turns, the target can repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on a success.

At Higher Levels. When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st. The creatures must be within 30 feet of each other when you target them.
